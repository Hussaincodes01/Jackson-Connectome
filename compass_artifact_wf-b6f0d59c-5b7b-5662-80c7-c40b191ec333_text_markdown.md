# Fly Brains, AI Companions, and VTubers: A Full Research Report for a Hobbyist Builder

## TL;DR
- **The fly-brain repos (flyvis, flybody) cannot understand language, recognize emotions, or hold conversations — that is not what they do, and no fruit-fly connectome model can be the "brain" of a companion or VTuber.** flyvis is a motion-detecting model of the fly's *eye/optic lobe*; flybody is a physics ragdoll of the fly's *body*. Your emotionally-responsive companion or autonomous VTuber will be built almost entirely from mainstream AI parts (an LLM, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, an avatar, memory), and the fly models can only add a genuine-but-cosmetic layer: an anatomically real fly avatar/mascot, a real fly visual system "watching" your webcam or stream to drive reactive twitches, or a live neuron-activity visualization on stream.
- **Both paths are very buildable in 2026 with open-source tools.** The fastest route is an existing framework — **Open-LLM-VTuber** (explicitly built to recreate Neuro-sama, MIT-licensed, runs local or cloud, Live2D avatar, voice, can connect to live-streaming platforms), **AIRI** (open-source Neuro-sama re-creation with VRM/Live2D and game-playing), or **SillyTavern** (best text companion frontend). A minimum viable companion is a weekend; an autonomous VTuber that manages its own YouTube is a multi-month project.
- **The legal/ethics layer is now real, not theoretical.** California's SB 243 (effective Jan 1, 2026) regulates "companion chatbots" with **no hobbyist exemption**, mandates AI-not-human disclosure, self-harm crisis protocols, and carries a **private right of action for the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation**; the EU AI Act Article 50 (from Aug 2, 2026) requires disclosing users are talking to an AI. YouTube requires disclosure of realistic synthetic content and demonetizes mass-produced "inauthentic" AI content. You must disclose the persona is AI, never present it as a real woman, and only clone voices you have consent for.

## Key Findings

**1. What flyvis and flybody actually are.** flyvis (TuragaLab) is the official PyTorch implementation of Lappalainen et al., *Nature* 2024, "Connectome-constrained networks predict neural activity across the fly visual system." It is a *deep mechanistic network* of the fly optic lobe: 45,669 neurons and 1,513,231 connections across 64 cell types arranged in a hexagonal lattice of 721 columns, modeling the ~700–900 ommatidia of the fly eye, with only 734 free parameters (single-neuron time constants, resting potentials, and unitary synapse strengths). It takes **visual input** (movies/hex-mapped images) and outputs **neural activity** and, via a trained decoder, **optic-flow/motion estimates**. It was optimized on a motion-detection task. It is installable with `pip install flyvis` (tested on Linux, Python 3.9–3.12), ships pretrained models, and has Colab tutorials. It does not process text, audio, or emotion.

**2. flybody is a body, not a mind.** flybody (Vaxenburg et al., *Nature* 643(8074):1312–1320, 2025, "Whole-body simulation of realistic fruit fly locomotion with deep reinforcement learning"; Google DeepMind + HHMI Janelia) is an anatomically detailed MuJoCo model of a female fruit fly built from confocal microscopy — per Janelia, "67 body parts connected by 66 joints, amounting to 102 degrees of freedom" — with added phenomenological fluid and adhesion physics. It is a platform for reinforcement-learning controllers that produce walking and flight. It is released under Apache-2.0 and is mirrored in the Google DeepMind `mujoco_menagerie`. Installation has three tiers (core; ML extension with TensorFlow/Acme; Ray training extension). Its outputs are **motor actions and simulated physics/kinematics** — useful for animating a realistic fly, not for conversation.

**3. Do flies have "emotions"? A careful answer.** There is genuine, respected neuroscience (David Anderson & Ralph Adolphs, Caltech; Gibson et al., *Current Biology* 2015) showing flies exhibit "emotion primitives" — persistence, scalability, valence, and generalization — e.g., a shadow mimicking a predator induces a persistent, scalable state of defensive arousal that influences behavior for minutes after the threat has passed. But the researchers are explicit: this describes **internal brain states**, not feelings. As William Gibson put it, "Our work can get at questions about mechanism and questions about the functional properties of emotion states, but we cannot get at the question of whether or not flies have feelings." A 2025 eLife reviewed preprint extended this with open-field-test evidence for emotion-like states mapping onto a valence–arousal circumplex. **Implication for you:** this research is a legitimate, interesting *thematic hook* ("my companion's mood is modulated by a fly-inspired arousal state"), but it is not a route to real empathy or understanding — those come from your LLM.

**4. The Sept 2026 viral fly-brain wave — and the honest verdict.** On Sept 3, 2026, HHMI Janelia's FlyEM team, the Cambridge Connectomics Group, and Google Research released **MaleCNS v1.0**, the complete connectome of an adult male fly CNS. Per HHMI Janelia (male-cns.janelia.org), it contains **166,691 neurons annotated into 11,691 cell types** (Google Research's Sept 3 announcement rounds this to "over 166,000 neurons and 125 million synapses"), and notably only ~4% of neurons differ from the female in the sex-dimorphic wiring. It was published in *Cell* (2026) as Berg, Beckett, Costa, Schlegel, Januszewski, Marin, Nern et al., "Sexual dimorphism in the complete connectome of the *Drosophila* male central nervous system." Within days, hobbyists wired it into Minecraft (Evan Sinclair Smith's "NeuroCraft Fly," 166,700 neurons, 25,582,938 directed connections), Beat Saber, Doom, Super Mario 64, a crypto bot, an email classifier, and a "flytok" doomscroller — a meme trend covered by 404 Media, Gizmodo, and Dexerto. **Crucially, the honest projects found the wiring adds little:** water-bear86's **flytown** tests a real connectome as an LLM-swarm planner against shuffled-wiring null models and reports "honest nulls"; brandoncho369's **flybench** benchmarks against shuffled-wiring controls; and the widely-cited email-classifier reached only "~80% of the performance of a small conventional neural network" and collapsed past 5–10 categories. Every serious builder (and the creators themselves) stressed these are "an interactive way to explore a connectome, not evidence of consciousness."

**5. The real companion/VTuber stack is mainstream AI.** Neuro-sama (by Vedal) is the most successful AI VTuber to date — per Dexerto (Jan 2, 2026), citing TwitchTracker, she became "the most subscribed-to streamer on Twitch, overtaking every human creator," with roughly 162,000–167,000 active paid subscribers, far ahead of second-place human streamer Jynxzi (~73,942). She is fundamentally an LLM plus TTS, a Live2D/Unity avatar, computer-vision game agents, and — critically — a **moderation/filter layer** that Vedal rebuilt after a **two-week Twitch ban in January 2023** widely attributed to her uttering hate speech, including Holocaust denial. Vedal himself advises beginners to start with an LLM API. Open-source recreations (Open-LLM-VTuber, AIRI) confirm "the pipeline is the easy part": LLM + STT + TTS + avatar wiring is well-trodden; the hard parts are personality, memory, latency, and moderation.

## Details

### A. What the fly models can and cannot do

| | flyvis | flybody |
|---|---|---|
| What it models | Fly optic lobe (vision) | Fly body (biomechanics) |
| Paper | Lappalainen et al., *Nature* 2024 | Vaxenburg et al., *Nature* 2025 |
| Input | Visual stimuli (hex-mapped video/images) | Motor commands / RL policy actions |
| Output | Neuron activity; decoded motion/optic-flow | Physics simulation: walking, flight, pose |
| Framework | PyTorch | MuJoCo (+ TensorFlow/Acme/Ray for RL) |
| Install | `pip install flyvis` (Linux, Py 3.9–3.12) | conda + pip, three tiers |
| Compute | Runs on a single consumer GPU; ensembles want more | CPU for basic sim; GPU/cluster for RL training |
| Language/emotion/chat | **None** | **None** |

Neither model — nor MaleCNS, nor any current fly connectome — can process natural language, recognize human emotion, or converse. A connectome is a static wiring diagram; running it as a dynamical system (leaky-integrate-and-fire) lets signals propagate, but there is no text faculty, no semantic understanding, and no affect. The email-classifier demo worked by pattern-matching character sequences to templates, not by understanding English ("the fly doesn't understand English. It routes it").

### B. Where a fly model can genuinely add value (vs. pure aesthetics)

1. **Real fly avatar/mascot (flybody) — genuine + visual.** Use flybody in MuJoCo to render an anatomically real fly that walks/flies as a companion mascot or an on-stream "pet." This is genuinely novel eye-candy; the fly is not the mind.
2. **Real fly vision "watching" the stream (flyvis) — genuine, reactive.** Feed your webcam/game/stream video into flyvis and use its motion/optic-flow output to drive **reactive attention or twitches** in the avatar (e.g., the character/fly startles at fast on-screen motion, mirroring the fly's real T4/T5 direction-selective cells). This is a real, defensible use of the science.
3. **Connectome activity visualization — genuine, educational.** Render live neuron-activation heatmaps (à la the viral demos) as a stream overlay. Honest, engaging, and clearly labeled as visualization.
4. **Fly "internal-state" mood modulator — thematic, semi-genuine.** Borrow the Anderson-lab "arousal/valence" primitives to compute a scalar mood/arousal variable (persistence + scalability) that biases your LLM persona's tone. This is inspired-by, not powered-by, the fly — market it honestly.

**Verdict on novelty vs. aesthetics:** In every realistic architecture, the fly models are **peripheral and largely aesthetic/thematic**. The "understanding" and "emotion" come from the LLM and affective-computing components. Claiming a fly brain "is" your companion would be misleading and, for a companion product, potentially a legal disclosure problem.

### C. Components you actually need for an emotionally-responsive companion

- **LLM (the brain).** Local open-weight (via Ollama, LM Studio, KoboldCpp, llama.cpp) for privacy/no per-token cost, or cloud APIs (OpenAI/Anthropic/etc.) for higher quality. Roleplay-tuned local models are widely used (e.g., MythoMax, Nous Hermes, L3-8B-Stheno). A 7B–13B model runs on a 12–16GB GPU (e.g., the AMD RX 9070 XT at $599/16GB, or used NVIDIA cards); larger models want 24GB+.
- **Persona + memory.** Character cards (SillyTavern's PNG/JSON format), system prompts, and long-term memory via a vector database / RAG or MemGPT for "perpetual" chat.
- **Speech-to-text.** faster-whisper locally (~200ms streaming; ~1–2s on an RTX 4080, ~7s on old GPUs), or Deepgram (~150ms) in the cloud.
- **Text-to-speech (voice).** Open-source: **XTTS-v2/Coqui** (17 languages, 6-sec voice cloning, <150–200ms streaming, emotion/style transfer — but weights are non-commercial CPML); **Chatterbox** (Resemble AI, MIT, emotion control, clean commercial license); **Piper** (runs on a Raspberry Pi; note the maintained OHF-Voice fork is GPL-3.0); **Kokoro-82M** (efficient). ElevenLabs is the premium cloud option (~75ms).
- **Emotion recognition (affective computing).** Text sentiment via any LLM; **speech emotion recognition** via SpeechBrain / wav2vec2 / HuBERT / WavLM-based models (trained on IEMOCAP, RAVDESS, CREMA-D); facial emotion via OpenCV/DNN models. Multimodal LLMs (e.g., AffectGPT, Qwen2-Audio) increasingly fold this in.
- **Avatar.** Live2D (2D, via VTube Studio), VRM (3D), or Unity. Lip-sync from the TTS audio.
- **Frameworks that bundle most of this:** **Open-LLM-VTuber** (voice, Live2D/Cubism 5, vision, MCP tool-calling, live-stream connection, offline-capable; ~13,700 GitHub stars), **AIRI** (VRM/Live2D, voice, game-playing, 25+ LLM providers, WebGPU), **SillyTavern** (text/roleplay king; add TTS/STT extensions). Others: Nekro Agent, Soul of Waifu, Amica.

### D. AI VTubers: case studies, autonomy, latency, moderation

- **Neuro-sama (Vedal).** LLM-driven personality, TTS voice, a separate singing model using a cloned version of her voice, a Unity/C# avatar with (largely) pre-recorded motion loops, computer-vision game agents, and a moderation filter (word-filter/neural classifier) rebuilt after the 2023 Twitch ban. Reach (per Wikipedia): Bilibili "Vedal和Neuro-sama" 1.12M followers; Twitch "vedal987" 1.01M followers; YouTube "Neuro-sama" 916K subscribers / 409M views. **Autonomy is real but bounded:** the LLM generates responses live, but a human (Vedal) sets guardrails, curates, and intervenes.
- **Latency budget.** A voice pipeline is STT + LLM + TTS + network. Naive/batch = 1.5–3s (unusable); streaming (start TTS before the LLM finishes) targets <500ms–1s. Frameworks like Pipecat target a 300ms budget. Stack overhead compounds, so measure every stage.
- **Stream integration.** OBS for compositing; Twitch IRC/EventSub or the YouTube Live chat API for reading chat; VTube Studio API for avatar expressions. Vedal's official **Neuro SDK** (WebSocket, Unity/Godot/community ports) shows game integration is a typed, validated action protocol — games register action schemas, the AI picks validated actions. Works best for turn-based games; real-time games need higher-level action abstraction.
- **What must stay human-in-the-loop:** moderation of AI outputs (the #1 lesson from Neuro-sama), handling of donations/monetization decisions, DMCA/copyright choices, and crisis situations in chat.

### E. Autonomous YouTube channel management — feasibility and limits

The YouTube Data API v3 gives a default **10,000 quota units/day** (resets midnight Pacific). As of 2026, uploads got dramatically cheaper: `videos.insert` was cut from ~1,600 units to ~100 (Dec 4, 2025), then moved to its own bucket (June 1, 2026) at ~1 unit/call, capped at ~100 uploads/day. **The new bottleneck is search** (`search.list` = 100 units; ~100 searches exhausts the day). Reads = 1 unit, writes/updates = ~50, comment insert = ~50. So an autonomous agent can realistically upload/schedule videos, post community replies, and read chat within quota — but heavy discovery/search is constrained, and spinning up multiple Cloud projects to multiply quota **violates YouTube's ToS** (and Google detects and revokes access). Acting on a channel's own content requires OAuth 2.0. A genuine quota increase requires a compliance-audit request that takes weeks and can be denied.

### F. Platform rules and legal/ethical landscape (2026)

- **YouTube inauthentic-content policy.** Renamed from "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" on July 15, 2025 (a clarification, not a new ban), then clarified again in July 2026 with new categories including "AI Personas Related to Sensitive Topics" and "Unsatisfying or Offputting Content." Mass-produced/templated AI "slop" is demonetized; AI-assisted content with "significant original and authentic value" stays eligible. Enforcement is now channel-level.
- **YouTube synthetic-content disclosure.** Realistic AI-generated/altered content (synthetic voices, deepfakes, AI-written primary narrative) must be disclosed via the "altered or synthetic content" toggle at upload. Failure triggers a warning → 90-day monetization suspension → permanent YPP removal path; YouTube can auto-apply labels for repeat offenders. Clearly-animated/unrealistic content generally doesn't need it.
- **YPP thresholds.** Entry: 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (or 10M Shorts views in 90 days). **From Feb 1, 2027, new applicants need 8,000 watch hours (or 20M Shorts views).** An already-monetized channel skips the new gate.
- **California SB 243 (companion chatbots).** Signed Oct 13, 2025; effective Jan 1, 2026; adds B&P Code §§ 22601–22606. **"Companion chatbot" = an AI system with a natural-language interface giving adaptive, human-like responses meeting a user's social needs, sustaining a relationship across interactions.** An AI girlfriend/companion is the paradigm case. **There is no size or hobbyist exemption** — an "operator" is simply "a person who makes a companion chatbot platform available to a user in the state" (functional exclusions exist only for customer-service bots, in-game bots that can't discuss sensitive topics, and stand-alone voice assistants). Requirements: (a) a **clear, conspicuous AI-not-human disclosure** where "a reasonable person interacting with a companion chatbot would be misled to believe that the person is interacting with a human"; (b) a **self-harm/suicidal-ideation protocol** with crisis-hotline/text-line referral, **published on the operator's website**; (c) for users the operator knows are minors: disclose AI, give a **break reminder at least every three hours**, and block sexually explicit content; plus a general notice that companion chatbots "may not be suitable for some minors." **Annual reporting to the California Office of Suicide Prevention begins July 1, 2027.** Enforcement (§ 22605, verbatim): "A person who suffers injury in fact… may bring a civil action to recover… (b) Damages in an amount equal to the greater of actual damages or one thousand dollars ($1,000) per violation," plus injunctive relief and attorney's fees — a **per-violation floor**, not a cap, with significant aggregate exposure at scale.
- **EU AI Act Article 50 (from Aug 2, 2026).** Providers must ensure users are informed they are interacting with an AI system, "in a clear and distinguishable manner at the latest at the time of the first interaction," unless it's obvious to a reasonable person. Open-source is not exempt; penalties up to €15M or 3% of worldwide turnover. Applies if your system's interaction reaches natural persons in the EU.
- **Other companion-AI regulation.** New York, Illinois, and Utah adopted disclosure/crisis rules; the federal GUARD Act (would ban companion AI for minors) and AWARE Act are advancing; China's interim measures (effective July 15, 2026) require easy exit and emotional-safety guardrails, and led some platforms to shut companion features. Context: the Sewell Setzer case and an FTC inquiry drove much of this.
- **Voice-cloning consent.** Cloning a real person's voice requires their explicit consent; clone only your own voice or a licensed/consented one. Mind TTS licenses (XTTS-v2 weights are non-commercial CPML; prefer Chatterbox/Piper/Kokoro for commercial use).
- **Ethics of simulated fly brains.** As of 2026 there is genuine (if largely non-alarmed) debate. The consensus in reporting is that MaleCNS simulations are "not capable of sentience or conscious perception in any tangible biological sense" — a wiring map without the organism's biochemistry. Serious commentators (and a 2026 *AJOB Neuroscience* piece) treat fly-simulation sentience as an open but currently low-probability question worth not dismissing entirely. Practically: running flyvis/flybody raises no meaningful welfare concern today, but avoid overclaiming.

## Recommendations

**Stage 0 — Decide the honest pitch (do this first).** Pick a framing that is true: "An AI companion/VTuber with a real fruit-fly brain *visualization* and a fly-vision *reactive layer*." Do **not** market "a fly brain that understands your emotions" or "a real woman." This choice drives your legal disclosures.

**Stage 1 — Minimum viable (1–2 weeks).**
- Path A (companion): Install **SillyTavern** + a local backend (Ollama/KoboldCpp) with a roleplay model, or **Open-LLM-VTuber** for voice + Live2D. Write a character card. Add faster-whisper (STT) and XTTS-v2 or Chatterbox (TTS). You now have a talking, remembering companion.
- Path B (VTuber): Use **Open-LLM-VTuber** or **AIRI**, connect a Live2D/VRM avatar via VTube Studio, pipe audio to OBS, read Twitch/YouTube chat. Stream *manually* with you moderating.
- Fly novelty (either path): add a **live flyvis or MaleCNS neuron-activity overlay** in OBS. This is the cheapest, most honest way to make the fly real on screen.
- Benchmark to advance: stable <1–2s response latency; the moderation filter blocks obvious bad outputs; you're comfortable with the persona.

**Stage 2 — Intermediate (1–2 months).**
- Add the **flyvis reactive-vision layer**: webcam/stream video → flyvis motion output → avatar startle/attention. Add a **flybody fly mascot** rendered in a corner.
- Add long-term memory (vector DB/MemGPT), speech-emotion recognition (SpeechBrain) feeding a mood variable, and a fly-inspired arousal/valence modulator biasing tone.
- Wire the **YouTube Data API (OAuth)** for semi-autonomous posting: the bot drafts titles/descriptions/replies; **you approve** uploads and community replies. Stay within 10k units/day; avoid search-heavy loops.
- Benchmark to advance: it can run a 1–2 hour stream with minimal intervention; disclosures are in place; no policy strikes.

**Stage 3 — Ambitious (3–6+ months).**
- Fuller autonomy: continuous inference loop, autonomous topic selection, scheduled uploads, automated (but rule-bounded) community replies, and game-playing via a Neuro-SDK-style action protocol.
- Robust **two-layer moderation** (input + output filtering) — non-negotiable before unsupervised streaming.
- Consider a custom fine-tuned persona model and a distinctive consented/original cloned voice.
- Keep humans in the loop for moderation escalation, monetization, and copyright.

**Legal/compliance checklist (do before any public launch):**
1. Persistent, clear "I am an AI, not a human" disclosure (SB 243 + EU Art. 50).
2. Never present the persona as a real woman; label AI content on YouTube via the synthetic-content toggle.
3. Self-harm protocol with crisis-hotline referral, published on your site (SB 243), if it's a companion.
4. Minor protections: break reminders at least every 3 hours, block explicit content, "may not be suitable for minors" notice.
5. Use only consented/owned voices; check TTS model licenses (avoid XTTS-v2 weights for commercial).
6. Don't multi-project the YouTube API; use OAuth; request quota increases legitimately.

## Caveats
- **The core reframing is the most important finding:** fly connectome models are not, and cannot be, the intelligence of a companion or VTuber. Anyone selling that idea is overclaiming; the Sept 2026 viral demos are fun but scientifically modest, and the honest ones (flytown, flybench) show shuffled-wiring controls often match the "real" brain.
- **Recency/volatility:** YouTube monetization and disclosure rules changed multiple times in 2025–2026 and will keep changing; the Feb 1, 2027 YPP threshold change and the July 2026 "inauthentic content" categories are the latest. API quota costs also shifted twice recently. Verify current policy at launch.
- **Legal specifics vary by jurisdiction** and this is not legal advice; SB 243's applicability turns on serving California users, EU Art. 50 on serving EU users. The "$1,000 per violation" private right of action is a real and significant exposure at scale.
- **Latency and hardware realism:** truly conversational (<1s) local pipelines need a decent GPU; older GPUs produce multi-second lag that breaks immersion.
- **Some sources are secondary** (meme trackers, blogs) for the Sept 2026 events; the underlying facts (MaleCNS in *Cell*, 166,691 neurons, dates) are corroborated across HHMI Janelia, Google Research, 404 Media, Gizmodo, Dexerto, and IBTimes. The Anderson/Adolphs fly-emotion science and the flyvis/flybody papers are from primary, peer-reviewed sources.
